    How it works

    Four steps from sign-up to result

    1

    Create your account

    Sign up with your index number and a password. It takes under a minute.

    2

    Apply

    Add your personal details and rank three schools while applications are open. You can edit until they close.

    3

    Marks are uploaded

    Your examination marks are loaded by the administrator. You never type them, so they cannot be changed by anyone else.

    4

    Selection and results

    Candidates are placed in three rounds. When results are published, sign in to see your school.

    Round 1Everyone's 1st choice

    For each school, candidates who chose it first are ranked by aggregate. The highest fill the seats.

    Round 22nd choice for the rest

    Candidates still without a seat are considered for their 2nd choice, again highest aggregate first.

    Round 33rd choice, last chance

    The remaining seats go to the best of those who chose that school third. Nobody is placed twice.

    Schools

    Schools you can choose from

    Every school has a fixed number of seats.

    Questions

    Things candidates ask

    Who enters my marks?

    The system administrator uploads marks from the examination records. You only enter your personal details and your school choices, so nobody can type in a better score.

    Can I change my school choices?

    Yes, as many times as you like while applications are open. After the closing date your application is locked.

    When will I see my result?

    After selection runs, the administrator publishes the results. The expected date is shown under key dates, and your dashboard shows your result the moment it is published.

    I forgot my password. What do I do?

    Ask the administrator to reset it. You will be given a temporary password to sign in with.

    What if I am not placed?

    Your dashboard will say so. It means the seats in all three schools you chose went to candidates with higher aggregates. Contact your sub-county education office about other options.
